# E-Commerce Sales Analysis | Interactive Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was developed during my Data Science internship at IDS. It focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data and transforming business data into interactive Power BI dashboards.

The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, product revenue, customer geography, and delivery status to support data-driven business decisions.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

**Visualizations:**
- Total Revenue KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Average Order Value (AOV) KPI
- Monthly Revenue Trend (Line Chart)

**Key Insights:**
- Identifies monthly revenue patterns and seasonal fluctuations.
- Highlights peak and low-performing revenue months.
- Supports analysis of order volume and average spending per order.

### 2. Product Performance

Analyzes revenue across product categories and individual products.

**Visualizations:**
- Category Revenue (Clustered Column Chart)
- Product-Level Item Revenue (Clustered Column Chart)

**Key Insights:**
- Identifies top-performing product categories.
- Highlights products contributing to overall revenue.
- Helps identify opportunities for product and category growth.

### 3. Regional & Delivery Dashboard

Explores geographical sales distribution and order delivery performance.

**Visualizations:**
- Revenue by Customer State (Map)
- Revenue by Customer City (Clustered Bar Chart)
- Delivery Status Distribution (Donut Chart)
- Customer State Slicer
- Date Range Slicer
- Revenue KPI
- Additional Order-Related KPI

**Key Insights:**
- Compares revenue across geographical regions.
- Identifies high-performing cities and states.
- Provides an overview of delivery status distribution.
- Enables filtering by customer state and date.

## Data Model

The dashboard uses a data model consisting of five core tables:

| Table | Purpose |
|---|---|
| fact_orders | Order-level transactions, revenue, and delivery status |
| dim_customers | Customer information, order count, and AOV |
| dim_date | Date dimension for time-based analysis |
| dim_products | Product information and category relationships |
| fact_order_items | Item-level revenue analysis |

Additional findings from the analysis:

- Beauty & Health is the leading revenue category.
- São Paulo is the highest-revenue city.

## Business Insights & Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, several business opportunities were identified:

1. **Seasonal Sales Planning:** Increase marketing investment during peak revenue months.
2. **Demand Stimulation:** Introduce targeted promotions during lower-revenue months.
3. **Inventory Optimization:** Prepare inventory ahead of expected demand peaks.
4. **Product Performance:** Protect and develop the strongest revenue-generating categories.
5. **Regional Growth:** Identify lower-performing regions with potential for expansion.
